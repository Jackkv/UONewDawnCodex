# Fame & Karma

Karma points range from -10.000 to +10.000.

Fame points range from 0 to 10.000.

Both use an exponential scale, so each tier requires dramatically more points than the last.

## Titles

Your reputation and skill titles only show once you hit the top two Fame tiers (5.000 - 10.000).

Karma doesn't affect visibility at all.

## Fame

A player only gains fame when fighting a creature that is more famous than they are.

The amount gained is equal to the difference between their fame values, divided by 100.

If the player is already as famous or more famous than the creature, they gain nothing.

When you die you lose 10% of your fame.

## Karma

All of the following actions will impact your karma negatively.

- Begging
- Snooping
- Killing animals like cats, dogs, dolphins, horses and pack horses/llamas.
- Poisoning
- Chopping Corpses
- Stealing
- Summoning Daemons
- Provoking NPCs
- Killing NPCs
