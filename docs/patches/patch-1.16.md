# SERVER PATCH 1.16

## August 31st, 2026

This is a smaller maintainence patch, though it does introduce some additional statues for you hunters out there.

## 🛠️ Bug Fixes & Enhancements

- Fixed issue with the reward vendor not giving you the new champ dye
- Fixed labels on large BODs
- Fixed graphic on the sturdy rolling pin
- Fixed issue where the tracking gump might not show sea life after a reboot
- Regular axes will lose durability/uses when used with lumberjacking
- Fixed material check on carpentry BODs
- Slayers should work on most monsters that spawn in rifts
- Several changes made to the loot drops, especially around magic weapons to lower the amount of magic weapon drops
- Scaled the single-click decay status message on ships to reflect 14 days, not 
- Fixed issue with 600-649 tailoring BODs potentially not returning a reward
- Fixed issue with the Move Hive functionality
- Added additional vines as rare drops
- Pirate Lookouts no longer teleport and will now come out of hiding when in range to attack
- Pirate Lookouts, if they have a bow, will attack from range
- Pirate map bard difficulty scales up slightly with the level you select
- Several vendors in Ocllo will only sell to young players (alchemist, blacksmith, carpenter, mage, and tailor)
- Due to the increased auction activity, I've moved auction messages to #auctions
- Non-Young players can no longer purchase items from the alchemist, blacksmith, carpenter, mage, and/or tailor
- Champ spawn no longer stand around forming that "square" spawn formation
- Spirit summoned pet hue has been slightly changed
- Fix incorrect graphic on one of the blacksmith weapon barrels
- Pirate smuggler bolt prices have increased slightly
- If you are ejected from a rift, your pets will come with you
- You can now auction addon deeds you craft from things like masonry
- Orc scouts drop arrows
- Fixed issue when NPC skill training (you might have 38.5 of a skill, but the menu would have that skill then grayed out)

## 🗿 Monster Statues

For all you monster statue hunters out there, you can now hunt for over 125 new statues that are now available (not including the already 70+ statues that existed already).

- All animals and monsters that have a statue are coded
- All monsters now have a chance to drop a statue, and an even rarer chance for that statue to be bronze, while the DOTW bonus continues to increase those chances
- Added an achievement for every statue type available
